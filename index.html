Welcome to my research webpage:)

I am currently (2024-2026) an ATER (Attachée Temporaire d'Enseignement et de Recherche) at the ENS de Lyon (LIP, PLUME team).

Before that, I was a PhD student under the supervision of Pierre Clairambault (LIS, team LIRICA) and Lionel Vaux Auclair (I2M, team LDP), at Aix-Marseille Université. I defended my PhD thesis titled Pointer Concurrent Games and the Resource Calculus on December 4th [pdf] [slides]

I am interested in programming semantics; in particular, I am currently working on the links between game semantics and Taylor expansions of lambda-terms.
