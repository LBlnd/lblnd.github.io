Research Interests:

I'm interested in denotational semantics, in particular game semantics -- where you see programs as strategies in a game. During my PhD, I worked on Pointer Concurrent Games, a game model developped in order to study the causal structures of innocent strategies. My PhD manuscript is available [here] and offers a detailed (and -hopefuly!- instructive) introduction to these games, as well as a presentation of their links with the resource calculus.

I wish to continue on this line of research and study the links between semantics and syntax, between different models of programming languages -- if these questions interest you as well I would be happy to discuss it by mail or irl!
